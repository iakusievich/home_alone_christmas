Stage 4 • 12–13 Minutes

Functional Language: Surviving Holiday Chaos

Learn natural A2 phrases for giving warnings, making polite excuses (white lies), and asking for help in an emergency.

Teacher Tip (Intonation):

Have the student practice stress and emotion! Warnings need urgent, high energy; excuses need a polite, calm tone.

1. Giving Warnings

  • Watch out for the icy stairs!
  • Be careful! That door handle is hot!
  • Don't touch that! It's a trap!

2. Polite Excuses & White Lies

  • I'm afraid my parents are busy right now.
  • Actually, my dad is in the shower.
  • Sorry, but I can't open the door for strangers.

3. Asking for Urgent Help

  • Could you please help me? It's an emergency!
  • I urgently need a ticket to Chicago!
  • Please send someone to 671 Lincoln Boulevard!

Bonus 1-on-1 Live Role-Play (2 Minutes)

Choose one scenario to act out live with your teacher!

Scenario A: Airport Emergency

Student = Kate (Kevin's Mom) | Teacher = Airport Ticket Agent

Goal: You are in Paris, and your 8-year-old son is home alone in Chicago! Convince the ticket agent to give you a seat on the next flight. Use: "It's an emergency!", "I urgently need...", "Could you please help me?"

Scenario B: Suspicious Stranger

Student = Kevin | Teacher = Harry (The Burglar at the Door)

Goal: Harry knocks on the door pretending to be a police officer checking the neighborhood. Do NOT open the door! Make polite excuses so he thinks your parents are home. Use: "I'm afraid...", "Actually, my dad is...", "Sorry, but..."
